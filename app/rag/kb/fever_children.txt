Title: Fever in children and babies
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/fever-in-children

Fever is common in children and is usually caused by a viral infection. Most children with a fever recover at home, but babies and young children need extra care because they can become unwell quickly.

A baby younger than 3 months with a fever of 38°C or above should be seen by a doctor urgently, even if the baby looks well.

Care at home for older babies and children:
- Offer plenty of fluids. Keep breastfeeding or bottle feeding as usual.
- Dress the child in light clothing. Do not wrap a child up warmly and do not sponge with cold water.
- Watch the child closely and keep checking on them, including during the night.

Get urgent medical help if the child is very sleepy or hard to wake, is floppy, is breathing fast or with difficulty, has a rash that does not fade, has a stiff neck, has a seizure, is not drinking or has not passed urine for many hours, or has cold hands and feet along with the fever. Ask a doctor or pharmacist before giving any medicine to a child.
