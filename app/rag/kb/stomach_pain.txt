Title: Stomach pain (abdominal pain)
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/stomach-ache

Mild stomach pain is common and often settles by itself. Causes include indigestion, gas, constipation, food that did not agree with you, and stomach infections. It can also be a sign of something more serious, so the pattern of the pain matters.

What may help with mild pain at home:
- Rest and drink small sips of water or clear fluids.
- Eat light, plain food once you feel able to, and avoid very oily, spicy or heavy meals.
- Notice when the pain started, where it is, and whether it comes with fever, vomiting or diarrhoea.

See a doctor if the pain lasts more than a few days, keeps coming back, or comes with weight loss, a swollen belly or changes in bowel habits. A general physician is a good first doctor to see. A gastroenterologist treats stomach and digestive problems.

Get urgent medical help if the pain is sudden and severe, the belly is hard or very tender, there is vomiting blood, black or bloody stools, a high fever, yellow skin or eyes, trouble breathing, or if the pain happens in pregnancy. Pain with sweating, breathlessness or chest pressure can be a heart problem and is an emergency.
