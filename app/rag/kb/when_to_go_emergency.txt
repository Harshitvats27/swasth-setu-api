Title: When to call 108 or 112 and go to an emergency department
Source: https://112.gov.in

In India you can call 112 (national emergency number) or 108 (ambulance) for medical emergencies. Do not wait or try home remedies if someone has any of these signs.

Call for emergency help right away for:
- Chest pain, pressure or tightness, especially with sweating, breathlessness or pain spreading to the arm, jaw or back.
- Trouble breathing or being unable to speak in full sentences.
- Signs of a stroke: face drooping on one side, weakness or numbness in an arm or leg, slurred speech or sudden confusion. Note the time the signs started.
- Loss of consciousness, fainting that does not recover quickly, or a seizure.
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with firm pressure.
- A severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face or throat, or trouble breathing.
- Suspected poisoning or overdose.
- Severe burns, a serious accident or head injury, especially with vomiting or drowsiness.
- Sudden severe headache, or sudden severe pain anywhere.

While waiting for help, stay calm, keep the person still and comfortable, and do not give food or drink. If the person is not breathing, start CPR if you know how. You can also use the SOS feature in this app.
