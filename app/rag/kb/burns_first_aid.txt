Title: First aid for burns and scalds
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/burns-and-scalds

For a minor burn or scald, cool the burn as soon as possible under cool or lukewarm running water for about 20 minutes. Do not use ice, iced water or very cold water, as this can make the injury worse.

After cooling:
- Gently remove rings, watches or tight clothing near the burn before it swells, unless the clothing is stuck to the skin.
- Cover the burn loosely with cling film or a clean, non-fluffy cloth.
- Do not apply butter, oil, toothpaste, creams or home remedies. Do not burst blisters.
- Keep the person warm, but keep the burnt area uncovered by blankets.

Go to a hospital or call 108 or 112 for a burn that is large, deep or blistering, or on the face, hands, feet, genitals or a joint. Also get urgent help for burns in babies, children, older people or pregnant women, burns caused by chemicals or electricity, burns from fire with smoke inhalation, or if the person has trouble breathing.
