Title: Safe water, food and hand hygiene
Source: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/diarrhoeal-disease

Many stomach infections, including diarrhoea, spread through dirty water, unsafe food and unwashed hands. They can be prevented with a few simple steps.

- Wash your hands with soap and water after using the toilet, after cleaning a child, before cooking and before eating.
- Drink safe water. If you are not sure the water is clean, boil it and let it cool, or use a safe filter.
- Wash fruit and vegetables well, and cook food thoroughly. Keep raw and cooked food apart and eat freshly cooked food.
- Keep food covered and stored properly, and keep kitchens and toilets clean.
- Use clean toilets and dispose of waste safely.

If someone in the family has diarrhoea or vomiting, wash hands often, keep their utensils clean and make sure they drink plenty of fluids, including ORS. See a doctor if there is blood in the stool or signs of dehydration.
