Title: Healthy daily habits
Source: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity

Small daily habits protect health over the long term and lower the risk of heart disease, diabetes, high blood pressure and some cancers.

Be active: adults are advised to do at least 150 minutes of moderate physical activity such as brisk walking every week, spread across the week, and to move more and sit less during the day.

Eat well: choose fruit, vegetables, pulses and whole grains. Limit salt, sugar and fried or packaged foods. Drink enough clean water through the day.

Sleep and mind: most adults need about 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Take breaks, spend time with family and friends, and talk to someone or see a doctor if you feel low or anxious for a long time.

Avoid harm: do not use tobacco in any form and limit alcohol.

Prevention: wash your hands with soap, keep vaccinations up to date, and get regular health check-ups, especially if you are over 40 or have a family history of diabetes, high blood pressure or heart disease.
