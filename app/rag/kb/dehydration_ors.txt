Title: Dehydration and oral rehydration solution (ORS)
Source: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/diarrhoeal-disease

Dehydration happens when the body loses more water and salts than it takes in. It is common with diarrhoea, vomiting, fever and hot weather, and it is most dangerous in babies, young children and older people.

Common signs of dehydration are thirst, a dry mouth, dark yellow urine or passing urine less often, tiredness and dizziness. In children, look for sunken eyes, restlessness or irritability, drinking eagerly, and being unusually sleepy.

ORS (oral rehydration solution) is a mixture of clean water, sugar and salt. It replaces the water and salts lost from the body. ORS packets are sold at pharmacies and are available at many health centres.

How to use ORS:
- Mix the packet exactly as the instructions on the packet say, using clean water. Do not add extra salt or sugar.
- Give small sips often, especially if there is vomiting. Keep going even if the person vomits once.
- Keep feeding children and keep breastfeeding babies during diarrhoea.

Get medical help if there is blood in the stool, the person cannot drink or keeps vomiting, is very sleepy or limp, passes no urine for many hours, has sunken eyes, or if the diarrhoea does not improve after a couple of days.
