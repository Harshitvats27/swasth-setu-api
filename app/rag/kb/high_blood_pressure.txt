Title: High blood pressure (hypertension)
Source: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/high-blood-pressure-hypertension

High blood pressure means the force of blood against the walls of your arteries stays higher than it should be. Over time it increases the risk of heart attack, stroke and kidney problems.

Most people with high blood pressure have no symptoms, so the only way to know is to have it measured. A doctor decides whether blood pressure is high, usually after repeated readings. Adults should get their blood pressure checked regularly, especially if they are older, overweight or have a family history of high blood pressure, diabetes or heart disease.

Lifestyle habits that help keep blood pressure healthy:
- Eat less salt and fewer processed and fried foods. Eat more fruit, vegetables and whole grains.
- Be physically active on most days.
- Keep a healthy weight.
- Avoid tobacco and limit alcohol.
- Sleep well and find ways to reduce stress.

People who have been prescribed treatment should keep following their doctor's advice and not stop on their own. Get emergency help for a sudden severe headache, chest pain, trouble breathing, vision problems, weakness on one side of the body or confusion.
